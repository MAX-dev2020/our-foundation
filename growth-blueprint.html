The load-bearing wall

Inward & inclusive

We don’t take sides on issues and we don’t moralise. We show the human nature underneath them, so anyone, on any side, can look at it and turn inward.

Rule

Observe, don’t judge

Curious, never “you’re living wrong”.

Rule

Show, don’t tell

People get there on their own.

Rule

No stands

Go underneath the debate, not into it.

Rule

No guru, no label

Any tradition can appear; none owns us.

02

Who it’s built for

design for the narrow,
package for the wide
EveryoneWelcome to find us: believers, seekers, the curious. The surface of every piece (hook, visuals, music) is made for them.
Seed audience · 19–25Skeptical, atheist or non-spiritual, intellectual but open. Quietly dealing with overthinking, comparison, loneliness, “what’s the point”.
Lever · opinion leaders 25–35The voices the seed audience follows. The depth of every piece is made for them to quote, stitch and share.

Two layers, one piece: the surface stops the scroll for anyone; the depth earns the respect of the people others listen to.

The content engine

ACollectProblems people talk about, and ones they suffer with quietly.
BScoreReach + Depth + Fit, each 1–5. Highest goes next.
CShapeTheir worry → the human pattern → turn inward → open question.
DMakeAs a song, a story, a reel or a post. Any world, any tradition.
Stream 1 · 2 a month

Trending

What everyone’s already arguing about. Brings reach.

“Different teams. Same need to belong.”

Stream 2 · 2 a month

Quiet suffering

Not famous, but felt alone by many. Brings depth and saves.

“Nothing’s wrong. Nothing feels alive either.”

Test before posting

Judged or curious?

If they’d feel told off, rewrite it. If they’d go “huh… wait”, it’s ready.

04

One piece, three cuts

make once,
publish 4–5 ways

Output: 4 core pieces a month become roughly 16–20 posts across platforms, with little extra work.

05

Where each piece lives

each platform
has one job
Home · depth

YouTube

  • Songs and music videos
  • Longer stories, calm music
  • Shorts cut from those
  • Found by search for years; ad income
Sharing · recognition

Instagram

  • Reels, carousels, quote posts
  • Stories and comment replies
  • Collab posts later
  • Where people send us to friends
Opinion leaders

X

  • Short lines and open questions
  • Threads on one topic
  • Replies to opinion leaders’ posts
  • Clips that link to the full piece

Daily habit: 20–30 minutes replying on X and answering comments on Instagram and YouTube. This is how the 25–35 voices find us.
